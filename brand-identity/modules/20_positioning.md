# Positioning Brief — Senior Engineer Portfolio

## Raw

> "I am a senior software engineer. I want a personal website that acts as my digital resume to attract clients."
> "Not generic yet not TOO flashy — subtle animations and movements are nice."
> Strategic tension named explicitly: **Memorability × Hireability**

## Synthesis

### Identity / Aesthetic Register
Senior software engineer operating as an independent consultant. Presents as **capability-led with editorial restraint** — proof of craft through the site itself, not through decorative spectacle. The site is both resume and demonstration of engineering discipline.

### Offer
Senior-level software engineering for client engagements — architecture, full-stack delivery, platform work. The portfolio must make scope, depth, and engagement model legible within 30 seconds.

### Target Clients
- Founders and product leaders at startups/SaaS who need a senior engineer they can trust
- Engineering managers seeking contract or advisory capacity
- Companies evaluating technical depth before a discovery call

### Differentiator
**Enforcement-quality craft** — a locked design system, performance-first build, and subtle motion that signals seniority without gimmickry. The site demonstrates how the engineer builds: disciplined, intentional, maintainable.

### Scoping Consequence
Weight **hireability signals** (case depth, outcomes, clear CTA) equally with **distinctiveness**. Aspirational references (Awwwards-level motion) inform craft ceiling; direct competitors (hireable senior engineer portfolios) define the realistic target.

### Strategic Tension
**Memorability × Hireability**
- Memorability pole: one ownable visual or interaction detail, strong typography, editorial layout
- Hireability pole: case studies with outcomes, services/availability clarity, enterprise-credible tone, fast performance, accessibility

### Candidate Formulations
1. *"The engineer whose portfolio proves discipline before the first call."*
2. *"Refined technical credibility — distinctive enough to remember, restrained enough to hire."*
3. *"Digital resume as living proof of craft: system, performance, subtle motion."*

### Open Questions
- Specific tech stack emphasis (backend vs full-stack vs platform)?
- Named client logos available for social proof?
- Productized engagement offers vs bespoke consulting?
- Geographic/timezone and availability signals?
